The island behind the prints

Prints

Every Berunda print starts somewhere on the map. Pull the three of them together and you get a rough tour of the island — the south coast, the hill country, and a crest that refuses to sit still.

  • Hikka Beach Paradise. A surfboard framing palm trees, a low sun and a turtle in the break, drawn in black line work on bright yellow.
  • Haggala Rise & Shine. A traditional rose over ribboned lettering, marked Haggala, Nuwara Eliya — hill country, printed on a pale tank.
  • Berunda Rules the World. Twin eagles with their wings out over a skull, ribboned lettering underneath, on white.

The line work is the thread. No gradients, no photographic prints, nothing that needs four passes to reproduce — just heavy black on a single colour, drawn so it still reads across a room.

All three are cut and printed in Sri Lanka, and all three ship islandwide. Browse the range.

About

Berunda was established in 2020 as a fashion brand. In fact, it is more of a lifestyle brand; it is all about the Future. The year 2020 was a challenging year Due to the COVID-19 pandemic. The nation united together to rise again and our youth led the way.
We value sustainability: zero plastic, recyclable materials, minimum wastage are our main concerns. We are motivated to work in various instances where we could help make the world to be a better place.
